### 🧠 What I do

At **[philosopht](https://philosopht.com)** I help founders take an idea from a sketch to a launched product, and help established companies replace fragile internal tools with systems that scale.

We work best with two kinds of people:

- **Founders** who need a senior technical partner to build and validate an MVP — not a body shop that bills hours.
- **Operators** stuck on spreadsheets, manual handoffs, and software that was good five years ago.

The output is the same in both cases: a real product, in production, with code you own.
